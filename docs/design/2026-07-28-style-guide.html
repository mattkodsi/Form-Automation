Palette

Drawn from a housing-authority file room — kraft, safety-paper green, received-stamp ink, ballpoint blue-black, the ruled blue of a columnar pad. Colour is scarce: roughly 90% of the screen is paper and ink, and the three meaning-colours are the rest.

Surface steps — the washed-out fix

The current app steps 4% from page to card, which disappears on a bright monitor. This steps about 10%. Depth is borders and surface shift only — no shadows. A document doesn't float.

--sunk · a ruled band, an input
--card · a document on the desk

Type — IBM Plex, two roles

Sans for everything the interface says. Mono only for figures that sit in a column being compared to another figure — rents, allowances, contract numbers. No display face and no italics: this tool files with HUD, and a typeface with a personality of its own reads as editorial. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and colour used together, which is more than enough. Scale steps at about 1.25 off a 13.5px body.

Provenance — not ours to restyle

These four are a contract with tests behind them. Colour answers is this saved?; the badge answers where did it come from? They become tokens at their existing values so night mode can tune them — the values themselves do not change.

The collision rule. Urgency and provenance may never share a surface. Urgency lives on the home page and launcher; provenance lives inside the form. The stamp red is a red no provenance state uses, so a deadline marker can never be misread as a save state. Check this before adding any coloured marker anywhere in the app.

The signature — one rule, three scales

On a cell it means provenance. On a row it means urgency. On a section it means status. One marker, learned once, true everywhere — which is why everything that competes with it goes quiet.

Controls and their states

Every control owes default, hover, active, focus-visible and disabled. Press feedback is a 0.975 scale. Primary buttons are never full-width — a solid slab out-shouts the page's statement.

Hover each, tab through them, and hold one down.

What this does not yet cover

The form's own surfaces: the nine-section rail, the command-centre bar, the unit-mix grid, dropdowns and source pickers, the dialogs, and the six-document package card. Those are the next section of the design, and none of them should be built until the values above survive your monitor.
